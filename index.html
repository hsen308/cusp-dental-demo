the clinic

Cusp is one dentist, one hygienist and one chair. You see the same face every visit — there is no rotating list of associates and no one you have never met finishing work someone else started.

Instruments come out of a sealed pouch in front of you, and the pouch has the sterilisation date printed on it. Ask to see it. Everyone should.
